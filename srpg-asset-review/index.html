8-Direction Unit Asset Review

Review 100 units per page. Mark KEEP / ARCHIVE / REJECT / REVIEW / BLOCKED, then export decisions.json for batch processing.

Decision guide
KEEPActive roster / story unit / boss / good asset to keep in game.
ARCHIVENot active now, but worth preserving in external archive.
REJECTFailed or unwanted asset. Still handled safely as archive/quarantine candidate.
REVIEWNeeds another look, rename, redraw, merge, or design decision.
BLOCKEDDo not auto-move. Referenced, missing, or parse/safety issue needs manual resolution.
